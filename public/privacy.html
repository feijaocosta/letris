Privacy policy

LETRIS, a Kingdom Apps game · last updated October 29, 2026

What stays on your device

Your best score, best level, control mode (buttons or gestures), language and your cookie choice are stored only in your browser. There is no sign-up, and we never ask for your name, email or any personal data.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics)

If you tap Accept, we use Google Analytics to understand how the game is played: how many games start, how far players get, which control mode and language they prefer, which version of the game they use and the type of device. This is statistical data that does not identify you. If you tap Decline, Google Analytics is not loaded.

Ads (Google AdSense)

The game is free and supported by Google ads. With your consent, Google may use cookies to show more relevant ads. Without it, only non-personalized ads are shown. We also show Kingdom Apps' own ads.

Learn how Google uses data on partner sites: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Changing your choice

On the game's start screen, tap Privacy (at the bottom, next to the version) and choose again. You can also clear the site's data in your browser settings.

Your rights

You can ask for information about how your data is handled or ask for it to be deleted. Since we do not keep personal data on our servers, statistics and ad data are held by Google under its own policies. Kingdom Apps is based in Brazil and follows the Brazilian data protection law (LGPD).
